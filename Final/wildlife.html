Park Wildlife & Seasonal Habitats

Located along the Mississippi Flyway, Moraine Hills supports migratory birds, turtles, deer, and prairie pollinators.

Bring binoculars in spring and fall for warblers, ospreys, and sandhill cranes, and watch for painted turtles along sunny shorelines.
